## Queasy Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Queasy is a collection of drug-free comfort routines for iPhone, Apple
Watch and Android phones. It does not require an account and does not
use HealthKit or Health Connect.

## Data stored on your devices

Check-ins, sessions, settings, reminders, and watch sync data are stored
on your devices. Analytics used by the app are local diagnostic events
only. Queasy does not upload your comfort logs, symptom descriptions,
device motion, or watch data to our servers.

## Purchases

Queasy Pro is offered through monthly and yearly auto-renewing
subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase. Apple processes payment
on iPhone; Google Play processes payment on Android.
RevenueCat verifies purchases and may process an anonymous app user ID,
transaction and entitlement information, and limited technical context,
including aggregate subscription performance. It does not receive your
check-ins, sessions, or health information. See
[RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

## Notifications and permissions

Notifications are scheduled locally. You can disable them in the app or
in your phone's settings. On Android, Queasy asks for the notification
permission only when you turn on Press reminders, and uses the vibration
motor and speaker or headphones only while a session runs. The app does
not require location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone recording,
HealthKit or Health Connect access.

## Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them in the app or remove the app.
Apple, Google and RevenueCat retain transaction records under their
policies. To ask for RevenueCat purchase data tied to your install to be
deleted, email the address below.
Email support messages contain the information you choose to send and
are used to answer your request.

## Children and changes

Queasy is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly
collect personal information from children. We may update this policy
when the app or its services change.
