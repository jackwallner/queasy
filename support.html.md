## My Apple Watch isn't buzzing

Open Queasy on your Apple Watch once so it finishes installing and can start
the wrist session. After that, send a pattern from your iPhone or start a
session straight from the watch. During a session, turn
the watch so its case sits on the inside of your wrist (about two finger-widths
below the crease, the P6 point) and keep the band snug so each pulse is easy to
feel.

## I don't have an Apple Watch

Queasy still works: tap **Use iPhone instead** and hold the
phone against your inner wrist. The iPhone plays the same pattern with its own
haptics, and phone sessions can add the 100 Hz comfort tone (headphones
recommended).

## Restoring a purchase

Go to **Settings > Restore Purchases** in the app. Make
sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you bought Queasy Pro with.

## Managing or cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Manage or cancel anytime in your
iPhone's **Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions**.
Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period
ends.

## Is Queasy a medical device?

No. Queasy is a set of drug-free comfort techniques you run yourself, and it
holds no regulatory clearance. It doesn't diagnose, treat, cure or prevent
anything, and it may not change how you feel. Acupressure bands and wrist
stimulation devices sold for nausea are cleared medical devices; Queasy is
neither, and is not a substitute for one. A watch tapping your wrist is not
acupressure and not nerve stimulation, and the app says so. If nausea is severe
or persistent, see a doctor.
