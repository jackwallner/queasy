When a car ride, boat trip or other journey leaves you feeling unsettled, choose a simple comfort routine you can do yourself. Start a mode directly, or answer three quick questions for a suggestion.

FOUR WAYS TO TAKE A PAUSE
- Breathe: follow a paced breathing rhythm through your phone's vibration, eyes closed if you like
- Pulse: rest your attention on a steady, regular tap; turn the level up or down while it runs
- Tone: listen to a one-minute 100 Hz tone through headphones at everyday volume
- Press: follow a guide to the wrist point an acupressure band sits on, and a three-minute timer while you press with your own thumb or a band you already own

THREE QUICK QUESTIONS
Say what kind of queasy moment you are in (travel, morning sickness, hangover, dizziness, nerves or illness) and how strong it feels. Queasy suggests a mode, a level and a length, and shows what each mode leans on, in the sources' own cautious words, so you can judge it yourself.

TRY THE WHOLE TOOLKIT FREE
All four modes are free and unlimited, with no account. Free Pulse and Breathe sessions last two minutes; Tone and Press always run their full length. Your last week of sessions, with how you felt afterward, stays in History.

Queasy Pro adds sessions up to 45 minutes, your full history with the mode you tend to feel better after, and Press reminders through the day. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in Google Play, and a one-time lifetime option is available.

These are drug-free comfort techniques, not a medical device or a treatment for any condition. A phone's vibration is not acupressure or electrical nerve stimulation. The app has no medical clearance, does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent anything, and may not change how you feel. If symptoms are severe, persistent, come with other symptoms, or you cannot keep fluids down, seek medical advice. In pregnancy, being unable to keep fluids down needs a midwife or doctor, not an app.
