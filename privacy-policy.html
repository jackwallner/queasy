Queasy Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Queasy is a collection of drug-free comfort routines for iPhone and Apple Watch. It does not require an account and does not use HealthKit.

Data stored on your devices

Check-ins, sessions, settings, reminders, and watch sync data are stored on your devices. Analytics used by the app are local diagnostic events only. Queasy does not upload your comfort logs, symptom descriptions, device motion, or watch data to our servers.

Purchases

Queasy Pro is offered through monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase. Apple processes payment. RevenueCat verifies purchases and may process an anonymous app user ID, transaction and entitlement information, and limited technical context, including aggregate subscription performance. It does not receive your check-ins, sessions, or health information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications and permissions

Notifications are scheduled locally. You can disable them in the app or iOS Settings. The app does not require location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone recording, or HealthKit access.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them in the app or remove the app. Apple and RevenueCat retain transaction records under their policies. Email support messages contain the information you choose to send and are used to answer your request.

Children and changes

Queasy is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We may update this policy when the app or its services change.
