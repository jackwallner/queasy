FREE CORE: No account or purchase is required. Pulse and Breathe have unlimited free 2-minute sessions. Tone and Press always run their full length. Pulse is a regular tactile rhythm, not acupressure or electrical nerve stimulation. Breathe is a paced-breathing guide. Tone generates a 100 Hz tone for one minute on iPhone at everyday volume. Press shows where the user can apply pressure with their own thumb or an existing band, then runs a timer; the watch does not apply pressure. Mode-specific source explanations and limitations remain in Learn. Severe or persistent nausea, other symptoms, or inability to retain fluids require clinical advice; pregnancy-related inability to retain fluids requires a midwife or doctor.
