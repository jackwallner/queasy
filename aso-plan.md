## Positioning

General nausea app, Watch-first. One app covers motion sickness + morning
sickness + hangover + vertigo, which multiplies the keyword surface while every
competitor picks a single niche.

## Wedge

1. Sense Relief is the only real Watch-vibration comp and it's beatable on
   quality (3.4★) and freshness. Its subtitle leads "Ease Nausea & Morning
   Sickness"; we take the general-nausea head terms plus the band tokens it
   doesn't own.
2. The 100 Hz sound-therapy wave (all <20 ratings) validates demand for
   drug-free nausea tools; Queasy covers vibration AND tone, so reviews/updates
   can chase "100hz" queries later without new metadata.
3. Hardware price anchor: Reliefband $100-250. Screenshot copy: "the anti-nausea
   band you already own."
